Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Sure Backups is a personal, single-owner tool. It is used only by the owner of grooms.cc and is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

The app connects to the owner's own Google Drive using the drive.file permission, which limits it to files and folders the app itself creates. It uses this access only to upload and manage encrypted backup files.

What is collected and shared

No personal information is collected from anyone other than the owner. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Backup contents are encrypted before they leave the owner's computer.

This website

This website is static. It has no forms, accounts, or analytics, and it does not set cookies.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account security settings.
